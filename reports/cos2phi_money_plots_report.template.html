The cos 2φ modulation on tensor-polarized ⁶Li: projected inclusive and coherent measurements

2026-08-17 · development run 5 · polligen (evgen/) + polli_fastsim (fastsim/) · companions: plans/06 (background budget), docs/note_cos2phi_coherent_6Li.md, reports/polarized_li_primer.html (pedagogical introduction) · external numbers verified against primary sources; this revision adds dedicated fact checks of the moment constraint and of the far-forward detection chain

Two projected EIC measurements of the cos 2φ azimuthal modulation on a transversely tensor-polarized ⁶Li beam: inclusive DIS with only the scattered electron detected, and coherent diffraction with the ⁶Li detected intact in its 1⁺ ground state. The modulation is driven by photon double helicity flip — the gluon-transversity sector, closed to quarks and to bound nucleons [1,2] — and has never been measured on any target. This note gives the projections, the assumptions behind each number, and the checks made on both.

The cos 2φ term requires the virtual photon to flip helicity by two units. No spin-½ constituent can absorb that — no quark, and no bound nucleon — so Δ(x,Q²) is purely gluonic at leading twist and vanishes for any nucleus that is only protons and neutrons [1]. At leading order it is generated by the gluon transversity distribution δG through an O(αs) photon–gluon convolution,

(Detmold–Shanahan sign convention [25]; conventions differ between papers). Δ has never been measured for any target; the only prior experimental document is a JLab letter of intent without a sensitivity projection [3]. Magnitude guidance is thin. The single moment estimate is the Sather–Schmidt bag-model calculation for the spin-3/2 Δ⁺⁺ baryon, ∫x Δ dx = −0.012 αs [4], which sets a per-mille scale for the asymmetries; percent-level numbers exist only for maximal-transversity (ΔTg = Δg) proton–deuteron Drell–Yan scenarios that their authors call optimistic [5]. We therefore work with a scenario band Δ/F₁ ∈ 10⁻³–10⁻².

2 · Machinery, statistics, and binning

Generator. No public generator produces polarized e+A events for A > 1 (BeAGLE is unpolarized; DJANGOH/PEPSI are nucleon-level and longitudinal-only), so polligen implements the missing piece — the spin-density matrix ⊗ structure-function master formula with spin-correlated sampling — and reuses everything else. Validation gates: bin-wise identity with the analytic asymmetry formulas at rtol 10⁻¹², pseudo-experiment spreads closing on every analytic error map, and unbiased cos 2φ recovery under φ-acceptance holes.

Full-luminosity statistics. For a binned estimator, drawing each φ-bin count from a Poisson distribution with the exact expected yield is statistically identical to event-level sampling, so a full-luminosity pseudo-experiment costs 24 Poisson draws (sample.phi_histogram_pseudo). Event-level sampling remains available and is what the closure tests use.

Estimator. The amplitude comes from a binned least-squares fit of N(φ′) = C(1 + A cos 2φ′), not the 2⟨cos 2φ′⟩ moment: with realistic φ holes the moment is biased by more than the signal, while the fit stays unbiased. Errors follow δA = √(2/N)/Pzz, validated by pseudo-experiments. The fit carries no cos φ′ term; the m-symmetric tensor fills set a₁ = 0 by construction.

Binning. The four displayed (x, Q²) super-bins are the top significance picks per Q² band (the raw map clusters at the lowest Q²; the Q² lever arm is kept because it is the consistency check against power-suppressed contributions, §6). Each super-bin merges 3×3 cells of the 40×30 analysis grid. Rates use per-nucleon luminosity, F₂ᴬ/A, scattered-electron cuts |η| ≤ 3.5, E′ ≥ 0.5 GeV, 0.01 ≤ y ≤ 0.95, W² ≥ 10 GeV², and the whole luminosity in the transverse-tensor fill; projections are quoted for the 1-year (10 fb⁻¹/u) and 10-year (100 fb⁻¹/u) programs.

3 · The Δ model and the moment constraint

What the constraint is. Sather and Schmidt computed the lowest moment of Δ in the MIT bag model for the spin-3/2 Δ⁺⁺ baryon and its Q² evolution; they give no x-shape [4]. The explicit αs(Q²) is the leading-order Wilson coefficient of the photon–gluon box, so the O(αs) suppression relative to F₁ is model-independent; the −0.012 is the bag estimate. Lattice QCD adds two data points, with heavy caveats: the bare φ-meson matrix element A₂ = 0.23(2)(5) at mπ = 450 MeV [25] converts to ∫x Δ|φ ≈ −0.006 αs (our conversion, unrenormalized), while the NPLQCD deuteron gives a δG moment of −0.010(3), bare at mπ ≈ 806 MeV [27] — a transversity-to-momentum ratio roughly ten times below the φ's, and implying the opposite sign for the deuteron's Δ moment. No calculation of Δ exists for ⁶Li or any A > 2 nucleus; the only nuclear model is a ΔΔ-isobar estimate for the deuteron [34], and the literature brackets nuclear Δ in both directions — suppressed as in the NPLQCD deuteron, or enhanced by binding like the EMC effect, as the polarized-ion white paper argues [50].

What we do with it. All Δ models live in one registry (polli_fastsim/delta_models.py). The production default, moment_A, is an ansatz normalized to the bag moment applied to the per-nucleon Δ of ⁶Li:

The constraint is imposed at the rate-weighted ⟨Q²⟩ = 3.9 GeV² of the accepted phase space; because F₁ evolves, the realized moment drifts between −4% and +7% across the plotted Q² slices. αs comes from the CT18NLO table (the LO analytic fallback runs 14% high). The solved A = −0.294 matches the EPPS21-based money_delta value −0.31 at its ⟨Q²⟩ = 7.4 GeV². moment_B applies the same constraint without the F₁ factor and lands a factor 2–8 lower; the A-vs-B spread is the dominant ansatz systematic, and both curves are drawn on every plot.

What it is not. Porting a single-hadron bag moment to a per-nucleon nuclear structure function has no literature precedent; both the normalization and the x-shape are labeled scenario choices, bracketed by moment_B below and by the possible nuclear enhancement above. What the projection then shows is that the measurement has the precision to decide: with the moment constraint in place, the x-shape separates interpretation A from B bin by bin.

4 · The coherent channel

4.1 · Detecting the intact ⁶Li at ePIC

In coherent diffraction, e ⁶Li → e′ X ⁶Li(g.s.), the recoil keeps the beam's magnetic rigidity exactly (A/Z = 2; the diffractive momentum loss xP < 0.01 changes it by < 1%) and emerges at θ = pT/p ≲ 1.7 mrad. Everything below 5 mrad enters the far-forward system: B0 spectrometer (5.5–20 mrad, charged tracks and photons above ~50 MeV), Off-Momentum Detectors (~30–60% of beam rigidity), Roman Pots at z ≈ 26/28 m (θ < 5 mrad, ~60–95% rigidity for off-rigidity tracks, AC-LGAD sensors with ~140 μm and ~30 ps resolution), and the ZDC for neutrals [7,8,9]. A beam-rigidity recoil rides inside the beam envelope and is invisible to all of them except the Roman Pots, and there only above the 10σ beam-envelope cut — at low |t| the intact recoil cannot be tagged at IP6 at all [10,51]. Where the cuts come from. The 0.20 GeV floor is the documented EIC requirement for high-acceptance (low-divergence) optics at top rigidity — divergence ≤ 73 μrad for 275 GeV protons [7,48] — and matches the one documented light-ion number, coherent ³He tagging limited to pT > 0.20 GeV at IR-6 [10]. The 0.45 GeV value is our derivation (150 μrad high-divergence proton optics applied to the 300 GeV ⁶Li momentum), not a documented number. No beam-optics parameters are published for any lithium beam; if the ⁶Li beam ran with proton-class divergence at this rigidity, the 10σ cut would land at 0.5–0.7 GeV and close the IP6 channel entirely. The cut is the single most consequential machine input to this measurement (§6, #1).

The IP6 fallback and the IR-8 fix. Standard EIC practice at IP6 is to establish coherence by vetoing breakup rather than tagging the recoil: dissociation protons (rigidity 0.50) hit the Off-Momentum Detectors, neutrons the ZDC, de-excitation photons the B0 calorimeter and ZDC, and ³He-like fragments (0.75) the Roman-Pot window [51]. Direct low-|t| tagging is the flagship argument for the second interaction region: its lattice adds a secondary beam focus ~45 m downstream where the beam is σ ≈ 0.15 × 0.1 mm, so Roman Pots at z = 44.0/45.5 m reach pT ≈ 0. The published intact-recoil efficiencies there are d 47%, ³He 32%, ⁴He 29%, ⁷Li 17.8% at top energy (rising steeply at lower energies — ³He reaches 99.8% at 5×41) [10]; no ⁶Li case has been published, and our interpolation is ≈20%. One degeneracy remains even there: a Roman-Pot hit measures only A/Z, so intact ⁶Li, α, and d at the same velocity are identical in trajectory and timing; the second-detector design addresses this with a proposed Z²-scaling Cherenkov behind the pots (R&D status) [8], while for the IP6 AC-LGADs no charge-discrimination study exists — signal amplitude is used for position interpolation only, and even the minimum-angle ion acceptance there is not yet fully established [49]. That question (open item #19) decides whether IP6 coherent-tag purity is event-by-event or statistical (§5).

4.2 · Rate model

No light-nucleus diffractive prediction exists (the lightest published is Ca), so the f₀ band brackets HERA ep diffraction (10–15% of DIS [44]) and heavy-A saturation estimates (20–25% [45]). xcoh = 0.01 is where the coherence length lc = 0.105 fm/xP drops below the ⁶Li size. The slope B = 50 GeV⁻² [40–60] follows from B = ⟨r²⟩/3 with the matter radius (the charge radius gives ≈57 [47]; gluonic distributions may be wider [42]); the exponential form is valid below the form-factor minimum at |t| ≈ 0.31 GeV² [11,12]. Writing f_coh against Bjorken x rather than xP is optimistic for high-mass diffraction; the f₀ band covers it. Yields: 1.1×10⁷ tagged events in year 1, 1.1×10⁸ over ten; best super-bin δA = 1.9×10⁻³ / 6×10⁻⁴.

4.3 · The anchored amplitude

The cos 2φ′ modulation of the tagged yield carries two mechanisms. The deformation term is scaled from the polarized-deuteron CGC calculation of Mäntysaari, Salazar, Schenke, Shen, and Zhao [13] — the only published calculation of polarization-dependent azimuthal coefficients in coherent diffraction on any polarized nucleus (all forward citations checked; the group's follow-ups are unpolarized [14,15]). Their digitized coefficients (coherent.MANTYSAARI_A2_DEUTERON) show a₂ growing linearly in |t| with a₂(m=0) = −2a₂(m=±1), from the 6% D-wave modulating the diffractive slope. For a fill with populations pm the ensemble coefficient is proportional to Pzz:

εB0 ≡ ΔB₀/B is scaled from the deuteron's 0.21 by two dials that disagree: the relative quadrupole deformation Q/⟨r²⟩ of ⁶Li is about 1/5 of the deuteron's and opposite in sign, while the α–d asymptotic D/S ratio is disputed — the transfer measurement gives η = +0.0003(9), consistent with zero [16], where older sub-Coulomb analyses gave η ≈ −0.01. We take εB0 ∈ −(0.04–0.13), default −0.08, with near-zero deformation open (the 5σ floors cover its detection or exclusion). The band is a physics statement: even exact few-body theory misses the ⁶Li quadrupole cancellation by ~3× [17], and the gluonic deformation need not cancel like the charge one. The sign flip relative to the deuteron is a testable prediction (Q(⁶Li) < 0). Existence proofs at this |t|: e-d T₂₀ = −(0.1–0.3) [19]; HERMES Azz(d) ≈ −1% at x ≈ 0.01 [20], coherent-double-scattering in origin [21–23]. The flat gluon-transversity term is separately bounded at 3×10⁻³–10⁻², from the bare lattice φ transversity [25] divided by the ~10× NPLQCD deuteron suppression [27] (both at unphysical pion masses), with the maximal Drell–Yan scenario [5] as the ceiling. The equal-rate population average behind ⟨a₂⟩ carries a one-sided ×0.73 rate-weighting systematic (audited; inside the εB0 band).

What the tagged sample measures: the two mechanisms separate by shape. Deformation is t-linear, sign-flipped vs the deuteron, and should weaken toward small x_P (JIMWLK evolution washes out deformation [15]); exotic glue is flat in t and survives at small x, where it connects to the elliptic-gluon program [6]. A two-component fit a₂(t) = c_def·|t| + a_g per x_P bin has both components ≥ 5σ above the statistical floor at 100 fb⁻¹/u. Incoherent breakup is m-state-blind in this mechanism [13]: it dilutes but cannot fake the modulation.

Extraction systematics: the modulation is self-normalized within a single fill, so relative-luminosity systematics drop out; φ-acceptance holes are handled by the binned fit; the polarimetry scale δP_zz/P_zz ≈ 3% is common with A_zz; radiative corrections for tensor observables are uncalculated and flagged on every plot. At the interpretation level, a ΔΔ-isobar admixture can mimic Δ at leading twist [34] and conventional double scattering generates the low-x b₁ [21–23]; the Q² lever arm and the ⁶Li/d comparison are the discriminants.

The ⁶Li null test: deformation-driven coherent modulations — the polarized-deuteron imaging signal [13] — are bounded on ⁶Li by its anomalously small quadrupole moment, Q = −0.0806(6) fm² ≈ Q(⁷Li)/50 [35]. A sizable flat-in-t cos 2φ on ⁶Li cannot be a shape effect; the mechanisms entangled for the deuteron separate in the ⁶Li/d comparison.

7 · Next steps

Near term: BeAGLE incoherent ⁶Li spectra to turn the veto efficiency and |t|-fit systematics into numbers (plans/02); VMC α–d/α–t overlaps replacing the two-parameter radial forms (plans/04); pseudo-experiment closure with a 2-D (φ, φ_t) acceptance map (plans/03); HepMC3 output and an ePIC chain smoke test (plans/05) so these projections can be repeated at reconstructed level.

Theory asks: rerun the IP-Glasma polarized-deuteron setup [13] with an α–d cluster density — the single highest-leverage input, replacing both ε_B0 dials and the f₀ band at once; a lattice Δ moment for a light nucleus; the spin-3/2 structure-function basis for ⁷Li; tensor-observable radiative corrections; the sum-rule tie between transversity-GPD moments and measured tensor form factors [30].

Machine and detector asks: lithium beam-optics parameters (they set the IP6 p_T cut, assumption #1); an answer to #19 — can the RP AC-LGAD amplitude separate Z = 3/2/1 at identical rigidity and velocity?; and the IR-8 secondary focus with its proposed Z² Cherenkov [8], which lifts the tag toward p_T ≈ 0 (⁷Li: 17.8% published; ⁶Li not yet studied [10]) — the coherent-⁶Li program is one more physics case for the second interaction region. Off-momentum halo sets how close the pots sit, i.e. the cut itself.
