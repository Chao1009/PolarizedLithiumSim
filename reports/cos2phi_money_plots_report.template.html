The cos 2φ modulation on tensor-polarized ⁶Li: projected inclusive and coherent measurements

2026-08-17 (development run 5) · revised 2026-08-26 · polligen (evgen/) + polli_fastsim (fastsim/) · companions: plans/06 (background budget), docs/note_cos2phi_coherent_6Li.md, reports/polarized_li_primer.html, reports/reconstruction_chain_report.html (the reconstructed level: money plots 5R, 7R, 6R) · modification history in Appendix A

This note presents two projected EIC measurements of the cos 2φ azimuthal modulation on a transversely tensor-polarized ⁶Li beam: inclusive DIS, requiring only the scattered electron, and coherent diffraction with the ⁶Li detected intact in its 1⁺ ground state. The modulation is generated by photon double helicity flip and probes gluon transversity [1,2]; it has not been measured on any target.

1 · The observable

For unpolarized electrons on a spin-1 target whose spin axis makes angle θm with the beam, the inclusive cross section per spin projection m is [1,2]

Sign convention of the tensor-rate terms. The b₁, b₂ terms are written in this program's transcription; in the Hoodbhoy–Jaffe–Manohar convention as applied by HERMES and by Cosyn et al. (W. Cosyn, B. Roldan Tomei, A. Sosa, A. Zec, Eur. Phys. J. A 61 (2025) 83, Eq. 27: Azz = −(2/3) b₁/F₁ for the axis along the photon) they carry the opposite sign and the m = 0 state has the larger rate for b₁ > 0. Only the sign of Azz relative to b₁, and of the constant term of the spin-state ratio, is affected; nothing in the Δ sector is.

The cos 2φ term requires the virtual photon to flip its helicity by two units, which no spin-½ constituent can absorb: neither quarks nor bound nucleons contribute at leading twist, so Δ(x,Q²) is purely gluonic and vanishes for a nucleus composed of nucleons alone [1]. At leading order it is generated by the gluon transversity distribution δG through the O(αs) photon–gluon convolution

(in the sign convention of Ref. [25]; conventions differ between papers). The only prior experimental document is a JLab letter of intent without a sensitivity projection [3], and the only moment estimate is the bag-model calculation of §3, which puts the asymmetry at the per-mille level; percent-level values arise only in maximal-transversity (ΔTg = Δg) scenarios for proton–deuteron Drell–Yan, described by their authors as optimistic [5]. The projections therefore span the scenario band Δ/F₁ ∈ 10⁻³–10⁻².

One finite-γ effect is not a labeling matter — the tensor-polarized b₁–b₃ terms feed the cos 2φ amplitude at O(γ²), bounded by γ²(b₁/F₁) × 1.15 — the full Cosyn Eq. (17e) combination, ≈ 6.9× the γ²b₁/6 of the b₁ term alone — which is at most 3% of the Δ amplitude at the sweet spots but exceeds a 10⁻³ scenario outright at x ≈ 0.2, Q² ≈ 1 GeV², where the amplitude-versus-x panels extend; it is computable from the same data's Azz (reconstruction-chain report §2). Structure functions are toy parameterizations with PDF-grid (CT18NLO/NNPDFpol11) backends behind one interface, so rates hold to a factor ≈1.5 and asymmetry shapes are scenarios by construction; R = σL/σT is likewise a toy of the right magnitude, 0.157–0.171 across the acceptance against 0.12–0.34 for the published SLAC/E143 R1998 fit, which sits behind an r_func hook. R is the one backend input that moves the physics result: F₂ cancels exactly in the cos 2φ amplitude (2×10⁻¹⁶), while Δ/F₁ = −2(1+R)Â carries R directly, so the published fit shifts Δ/F₁ by +16.6 / +18.0 / +4.7 / −4.4% at the four sweet spots — a systematic on every Δ quoted here.

2 · Machinery, statistics, and binning

Generator. No public generator produces polarized e+A events for A > 1 (BeAGLE is unpolarized; DJANGOH and PEPSI are nucleon-level and longitudinal-only), so polligen implements only the missing component — the spin-density matrix ⊗ structure-function master formula with spin-correlated sampling — and takes the rest from existing tools, the hadronic final state from a PYTHIA 8 sample of 8 M events over the three beam configurations. Its validation gates are bin-wise identity with the analytic asymmetry formulas at relative tolerance 10⁻¹², pseudo-experiment spreads that close on every analytic error map, and unbiased cos 2φ recovery under φ-acceptance holes. Since drawing each φ-bin count from a Poisson distribution with the exact expected yield is statistically identical to event-level sampling, a full-luminosity pseudo-experiment costs 24 Poisson draws (sample.phi_histogram_pseudo); event-level sampling is what the closure tests use.

Estimator of the figures. The amplitudes below come from a single spin state: a binned least-squares fit of N(φ′) = C(1 + A cos 2φ′) rather than the 2⟨cos 2φ′⟩ moment, which realistic φ holes bias by more than the signal while the fit stays unbiased, with δA = √(2/N)/Pzz validated by pseudo-experiments and no cos φ′ term, since the m-symmetric tensor fills set a₁ = 0 by construction. The figures below are these single-state, truth-binned projections.

Estimator of the measurement. A single-state fit also measures the detector's own cos 2φ′ acceptance harmonic divided by Pzz — a 3% harmonic on the vertical spin axis fakes four times the signal — so the estimator of the measurement is the spin-state-sorted ratio of m = ±1-rich (Pzz = +0.6) and m = 0-rich (−1.2) bunches (reconstruction-chain report §3). It cancels any acceptance common to both states bin by bin, turns relative-luminosity errors into a constant, and gives δÂ = 2√(2/N)/(P₊ − P₀) = 0.67× the single-state error at equal source purity, provided the states alternate bunch by bunch, or the harmonic is stable to 10⁻⁴ between fills: a 10⁻³ fill-to-fill difference of it fakes half the signal. At the reconstructed level — EMCal energy, track angles, εeID(η), hadronic-method y, reconstructed (x, Q²) bins — the four sweet spots give δÂ = 1.2 / 1.0 / 1.8 / 3.0 × 10⁻⁴ in year 1 (money plot 5R), 0.64–0.70 of the money-plot-5 annotations, and Δ (7R) reaches δΔ = 1.0×10⁻³ at Q² = 1.14 GeV² and 0.5×10⁻³ at 3.13 GeV². Two caveats. With the PYTHIA final state replacing the 25% Gaussian stand-in, sweet-spot purities are 0.40–0.73 rather than 0.64–0.68 and the reconstructed amplitude sits 4–21% below the true bin rather than 1–9%, at unchanged statistical error, on a Σ-method δy/y = 0.55 / 0.28 / 0.50 / 0.15 at this configuration and 50 MeV of calorimeter noise, degrading with beam energy, which puts the x ≈ 0.1 bins at low energy; the noise floor decides the y ≈ 0.01 bins, where E − pz is 0.2–0.5 GeV and no published ePIC study reaches. Bin-centering is model-dependent at (−5.0, +8.5, −9.3, +6.3)% at the four spots and 3–11% over the plotted bins, above the 10-year statistical errors; since the amplitude is exactly linear in Δ, the response folds as an exact linear operator, and fitting a Δ(x) shape through it per Q² slice reduces that to (−0.9, −1.2, −0.2, +0.3)%.

Binning. The four displayed (x, Q²) super-bins are selected by statistical significance within Q² bands — an unconstrained ranking concentrates at the lowest Q², while the Q² lever arm provides the consistency check against power-suppressed contributions (§6) — and each merges 3×3 cells of the 40×30 analysis grid. Rates use per-nucleon luminosity, F₂ᴬ/A, scattered-electron cuts |η| ≤ 3.5, E′ ≥ 0.5 GeV, 0.01 ≤ y ≤ 0.95, W² ≥ 10 GeV², and the full luminosity in the transverse-tensor fill, for the 1-year (10 fb⁻¹/u) and 10-year (100 fb⁻¹/u) programs.

3 · The Δ model and the moment constraint

Provenance. Sather and Schmidt computed the lowest moment of Δ in the MIT bag model for the spin-3/2 Δ⁺⁺ baryon, ∫x Δ dx = −0.012 αs, with its Q² evolution but no x dependence [4]; the explicit αs(Q²) is the leading-order Wilson coefficient of the photon–gluon box, so the O(αs) suppression relative to F₁ is model-independent while −0.012 is the bag estimate. Lattice QCD adds two results, both at unphysical pion masses: the bare φ-meson matrix element A₂ = 0.23(2)(5) at mπ = 450 MeV [25], which converts to ∫x Δ|φ ≈ −0.006 αs (our conversion, unrenormalized), and the NPLQCD deuteron δG moment of −0.010(3), bare at mπ ≈ 806 MeV [27] — a transversity-to-momentum ratio roughly ten times below that of the φ, implying the opposite sign for the deuteron's Δ moment. Nothing exists for ⁶Li or any A > 2 nucleus beyond a ΔΔ-isobar estimate for the deuteron [34], so the literature brackets the nuclear Δ in both directions: suppressed as in the NPLQCD deuteron, or enhanced by binding in analogy with the EMC effect [50].

Implementation. All Δ models reside in one registry (polli_fastsim/delta_models.py). The production default, moment_A, is an ansatz normalized to the bag moment applied to the per-nucleon Δ of ⁶Li:

with (a, b) = (0.7, 3); the factor 1/3 restricts the exotic glue to the polarized deuteron-like pair while the whole-nucleus Pzz remains a separate factor, so nothing is double counted. The constraint is imposed at the rate-weighted ⟨Q²⟩ = 3.9 GeV² of the accepted phase space, and because F₁ evolves the realized moment varies between −4% and +7% across the plotted Q² slices; αs comes from the CT18NLO table (the leading-order analytic fallback is 14% higher), and the solved A = −0.294 agrees with the EPPS21-based money_delta value of −0.31 at its ⟨Q²⟩ = 7.4 GeV². moment_B imposes the same constraint without the F₁ factor and yields amplitudes a factor of 2–8 lower; the A-versus-B spread is the dominant ansatz systematic, and both curves appear on every plot. Porting a single-hadron bag moment to a per-nucleon nuclear structure function has no precedent in the literature, so normalization and x dependence are both scenario choices, bracketed by moment_B from below and possible nuclear enhancement from above.

4 · The coherent channel

4.1 · Detecting the intact ⁶Li at ePIC

In coherent diffraction, e ⁶Li → e′ X ⁶Li(g.s.), the recoil retains the beam's magnetic rigidity (A/Z = 2; the diffractive momentum loss xP < 0.01 changes it by less than 1%) and emerges at θ = pT/p ≲ 1.7 mrad, inside the beam envelope. Of the far-forward system — the B0 spectrometer, the Off-Momentum Detectors (≈ 45–65% of beam rigidity, far-forward acceptance table of Jentsch, Tu and Weiss), the Roman Pots at z ≈ 26/28 m in the Yellow Report layout (32.5/34.3 m in epic-main; θ < 5 mrad, ~60–95% rigidity for off-rigidity tracks, AC-LGAD sensors with ~140 μm spatial and ~30 ps timing resolution) and the ZDC for neutrals [7,8,9] — only the pots can see such a recoil, and only above the 10σ envelope cut, so at low |t| it cannot be tagged at IP6 [10,51]. Provenance of the cuts. The 0.20 GeV floor is the documented EIC requirement for high-acceptance (low-divergence) optics at top rigidity, a beam divergence ≤ 73 μrad for 275 GeV protons [7,48], and matches the one documented light-ion number: coherent ³He tagging limited to pT > 0.20 GeV at IR-6 [10]. The 0.45 GeV value is our derivation, 149 μrad high-divergence proton optics applied to the 300 GeV ⁶Li momentum. No beam-optics parameters have been published for any lithium beam; with proton-class divergence at this rigidity the 10σ cut would fall at 0.5–0.7 GeV and eliminate the IP6 channel. This is the most consequential machine input to the measurement (assumption #1).

The near-beam cut is angular, and the pot aperture is measured. The 10σ envelope cuts the recoil angle (reconstruction-chain report §4), so the threshold scales with the beam momentum: pTcut = 10σθ·A·pu = 0.09 / 0.22 / 0.60 GeV at 40.8 / 99.5 / 137.5 GeV/u for the proton-derived σθ = 73 μrad, i.e. tag acceptances of 67% / 9% / 1.5×10⁻⁸ at B = 50 GeV⁻²; the constant 0.20 GeV of the figures (13.5%) is close to the mid-energy value and far from the other two. Independently of the envelope, the pots' own aperture for an intact ⁶Li has been measured by shooting one through the ePIC far-forward geometry (tools/fullsim): the boundary is |θx| ≳ 2.0 / 1.35 / 1.03 mrad in the 5×41 / 10×100 / 18×275 optics against |θy| ≳ 1.8–3 mrad, so the acceptance opens horizontally, where the chain's assumed cutout — wide in x, tight in y — opened it vertically; the cause is the transport, which images an IP angle onto the pot plane with a horizontal lever R₁₂ ≈ 30.6 m against a vertical few metres. A recoil must clear both constraints and the aperture is the larger everywhere. Carried through the chain at the low configuration: acceptance 37.7% → 1.42%, Ntag 8.3×10⁶ → 3.1×10⁵, acceptance-induced ⟨cos 2β⟩ +0.426 → −0.772, a change of sign; two of the four |t| bins survive (|t| = 0.061–0.273 GeV²) with δat worse by 6–34× (0.0014 → 0.0482, 0.0026 → 0.0150), and ae is still recovered at 0.0073 ± 0.0045 and 0.0091 ± 0.0045 against an injected 0.0100. At mid and top the aperture leaves no accepted recoil in the binned window: the coherent program is a low-energy program. The scan is one event per point in 30° azimuthal steps, without a beam envelope, on a September-2024 epic-main geometry (plans/04 #20). The coherent figures below predate the measurement and keep the constant 0.20 GeV cut; money plot 6R is on the angular envelope and that assumed cutout (|px| < 0.55, |py| < 0.22 GeV, with divergence smearing): 2.7×10⁶ tagged events at mid energy in year 1, a fake ⟨cos 2β⟩ = +0.77 cancelled by the ratio, at(tref) = 0.117 ± 0.007 for 0.119 injected and ae = 0.0101 ± 0.0013 for 0.010, for a background-free, Z-identified sample by assumption (the breakup budget is plans/06).

Coherence selection at IP6 and at IR-8. Standard EIC practice at IP6 establishes coherence by vetoing breakup rather than by tagging the recoil (§5). Direct low-|t| tagging is a central argument for the second interaction region, whose secondary beam focus ~45 m downstream (σ ≈ 0.15 × 0.1 mm) lets Roman Pots at z = 44.0/45.5 m reach pT ≈ 0, with published intact-recoil efficiencies of 47% (d), 32% (³He), 29% (⁴He) and 17.8% (⁷Li) at top energy, rising steeply at lower energies (³He reaches 99.8% at 5×41) [10]; no ⁶Li case is published and our interpolation is ≈20%. One degeneracy remains even there, since a Roman-Pot hit measures only A/Z and intact ⁶Li, α and d at the same velocity share trajectory and timing: the second-detector design answers it with a proposed Z²-scaling Cherenkov counter behind the pots, at the R&D stage [8], while for the IP6 AC-LGADs no charge-discrimination study exists (amplitude is used only for position interpolation) and the minimum-angle ion acceptance is not fully established [49]. That question, open item #19, decides whether IP6 coherent-tag purity is established event by event or statistically (§5).

4.2 · Rate model

No diffractive prediction exists for a light nucleus (the lightest published case is Ca), so the f₀ band brackets HERA ep diffraction (10–15% of DIS [44]) and heavy-nucleus saturation estimates (20–25% [45]); parameterizing f_coh in Bjorken x rather than xP is optimistic for high-mass diffraction, which that band is meant to cover. The scale xcoh = 0.01 marks where the coherence length lc = 0.105 fm/xP falls below the ⁶Li size, and the slope B = 50 GeV⁻² (band 40–60) follows from B = ⟨r²⟩/3 with the matter radius (the charge radius gives ≈57 [47]; gluonic distributions may be wider [42]), valid below the form-factor minimum at |t| ≈ 0.31 GeV² [11,12]. The yields are 1.1×10⁷ / 1.1×10⁸ tagged events in the 1- and 10-year programs, best-super-bin δA = 1.9×10⁻³ / 6×10⁻⁴.

4.3 · The anchored amplitude

The cos 2φ′ modulation of the tagged yield carries two mechanisms. The deformation term is scaled from the polarized-deuteron CGC calculation of Mäntysaari, Salazar, Schenke, Shen and Zhao [13], the only published calculation of polarization-dependent azimuthal coefficients in coherent diffraction on any polarized nucleus (all forward citations checked; the group's subsequent work is unpolarized [14,15]). Their coefficients, digitized in coherent.MANTYSAARI_A2_DEUTERON, show a₂ growing linearly in |t| with a₂(m=0) = −2a₂(m=±1), from the 6% D-wave modulating the diffractive slope. For a fill with populations pm the ensemble coefficient is proportional to Pzz:

Convention of the anchored coefficient. The anchor's Eq. (9) expands d²σ/dΦd|t| ∝ 1 + 2ΣnaneinΦ with Φ the azimuth of the produced vector meson — the momentum transfer, i.e. the recoil — relative to the polarization axis in the γ*d frame, so the cos 2Φ coefficient is 2a₂: the ⟨a₂⟩ quoted here and injected in money plot 6(d) understates the deformation modulation by a factor two (0.072 rather than 0.036 at Pzz = 0.6), and this report's coherent numbers are conservative by ×2 and kept in the a₂ convention. The modulation is one of the recoil azimuth φt − φS, not of the electron azimuth used as the axis of panel (d); plot 6R injects 2a₂ in the recoil azimuth and fits both azimuths (reconstruction-chain report §7).

εB0 ≡ ΔB₀/B is scaled from the deuteron value of 0.21 by two indicators that disagree: the relative quadrupole deformation Q/⟨r²⟩ of ⁶Li is about one fifth of the deuteron's and opposite in sign, while the α–d asymptotic D/S ratio is disputed — a transfer measurement gives η = +0.0003(9), consistent with zero [16], against older sub-Coulomb analyses favoring η ≈ −0.01. We adopt εB0 ∈ −(0.04–0.13) with default −0.08, leaving near-zero deformation open; the 5σ floors cover its detection or exclusion. The band's width is genuine theoretical uncertainty: even exact few-body calculations miss the ⁶Li quadrupole cancellation by a factor of ≈3 [17], and the gluonic deformation need not share the cancellation seen in the charge sector. The sign flip relative to the deuteron is a testable prediction (Q(⁶Li) < 0), and tensor modulations of comparable size are established at these |t|: e-d T₂₀ = −(0.1–0.3) [19] and HERMES Azz(d) ≈ −1% at x ≈ 0.01 [20], the latter attributed to coherent double scattering [21–23]. The flat gluon-transversity term is separately bounded at 3×10⁻³–10⁻², from the bare lattice φ transversity [25] divided by the ~10× NPLQCD deuteron suppression [27], with the maximal Drell–Yan scenario [5] as the ceiling. The equal-rate population average behind ⟨a₂⟩ carries a one-sided ×0.73 rate-weighting systematic, inside the εB0 band.

Separation of the two mechanisms. The two differ in shape: deformation is linear in t, sign-flipped relative to the deuteron, and expected to weaken toward small x_P (JIMWLK evolution washes it out [15]), while exotic glue is flat in t and persists at small x, where it connects to the elliptic-gluon program [6]. In a two-component fit a₂(t) = c_def·|t| + a_g per x_P bin, both components exceed the statistical floor by ≥ 5σ at 100 fb⁻¹/u. Incoherent breakup is m-state-blind in this mechanism [13]: it dilutes the modulation but cannot mimic it.

Two extraction systematics survive the spin-state ratio of §2: the polarimetry scale δP_zz/P_zz ≈ 3%, common with A_zz, and radiative corrections for tensor observables, which have not been calculated and are noted on every plot. In interpretation, a ΔΔ-isobar admixture can mimic Δ at leading twist [34] and conventional double scattering generates the low-x b₁ [21–23]; the Q² lever arm and the ⁶Li/d comparison discriminate against both.

The ⁶Li null test. Deformation-driven coherent modulations, the polarized-deuteron imaging signal [13], are bounded on ⁶Li by its anomalously small quadrupole moment, Q = −0.0806(6) fm² ≈ Q(⁷Li)/50 [35]: a sizable flat-in-t cos 2φ on ⁶Li cannot be a shape effect, and the mechanisms entangled for the deuteron separate in the ⁶Li/d comparison.

7 · Open inputs

The simulation chain's remaining gaps are plans/08 and the external dependencies plans/04. The highest-leverage single input is a rerun of the IP-Glasma polarized-deuteron setup [13] with an α–d cluster density, which would replace both εB0 indicators and the f₀ band at once; a lattice Δ moment for a light nucleus, or a measured tensor form factor read through the sum rule tying it to transversity-GPD moments [30], would pin the normalization of §3.
