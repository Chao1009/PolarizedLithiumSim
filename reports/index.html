Report 3
2026-08-26 · detector study

Superconducting nanowires at the ePIC far-forward: what a closer approach would buy the polarized-lithium tags

The coherent intact-⁶Li tag and the ⁶Li α tag are beam-blind, so the transverse angle at the IP is the only handle and the acceptance is an exponential in its square. This note prices every aperture (×26 at 5 × 41, ×569 at 10 × 100, and at mid energy the difference between a measurement and a non-measurement), then asks whether a superconducting nanowire can deliver it. The answer is no, in both candidate roles, on arithmetic rather than unknowns: a nanowire's dead edge is worth 0.005 mrad against a 0.5–3 mrad gap, and for charge identification the incumbent AC-LGAD already digitises an 8-bit charge over four planes — 4.8σ of α ↔ ⁶Li separation per plane against a nanowire's one bit. What survives is worth more than the negative answer: a technology-independent pricing curve, the finding that the ePIC pot geometry has moved since the snapshot this repository measured, and a redirected open question #19 — whose background separates by hit topology, 13 to 90 pixels apart, in sensors that already exist.
