Summary and Takeaways
• Far-forward physics characterized by exclusive+diffractive final states.
• Lots to unpack! – proton spin, neutron structure, saturation, partonic imaging,
meson structure, etc.

• There is lots of interest in the EIC community in studying this physics via
these final states!
• Exciting time to get involved à Need the detectors and software to do any physics!
