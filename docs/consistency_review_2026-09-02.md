## 1. Summary

**What was reviewed.** The five circulate-able reports (0 the primer, 1 the gluonometry projections, 2 the simulation and reconstruction chain, 3 the EIC/ePIC reference, 4 the near-beam tagging study), read against each other and against the ground truth of the repository: `README.md`, `plans/00_README.md` (runs 13–16), `docs/reproduction_manual.md`, plans/04, 08, 09 and 10, the fastsim and polligen code, and the scripts that produced every quoted number. The mechanical sweep (`tools/consistency_check.py`, 25 checks — beam energies, figure staleness, report numbering, run-plan shares) passes; this review is the semantic layer it cannot see. The reference sources on disk (39 PDFs under `refs/`, the Yellow Report tables, EPIOS, Hamwi, TUNL, ZEUS, Cosyn) were opened by the completeness critic.

**Method.** Two rounds. The first (2026-08-30/31, Fable throughout) built a claim ledger per report — quantitative claims, decisions and assumptions, cross-references, internal consistency — then ran per-report, per-pair and per-topic comparisons; 270 raw entries merged into 117 findings, F001–F117. The second (2026-09-01/02) put Opus finders on the lenses the first round lacked — arithmetic, assumptions, figures, script outputs re-run against the text, cross-references, and the ancillary documents (README, manual, index.html, plans) — and produced F118–F254; a completeness critic (three passes, `critic.md`) added C001–C006 and closed the external-source lens. Every finding then went to an Opus refuter (its brief: prove the finding wrong) and an independent Opus adjudicator (its brief: name the stale side from the primary sources), and the pair's verdicts were classified by rule into *agree* (both say real), *conflict* (refuted as a contradiction but a stale or defective passage named), *soft* (no contradiction, nothing stale, a wording change proposed) and *missing*. A judge then ruled on each finding from the primary sources, re-running the producing script where a number was in question: Fable, singly, for the blocking finding, the first-round majors, the round-2 majors whose two verify agents disagreed, the critic's findings and the unresolved classes; Opus, three findings per agent grouped by report, for the other verified findings; and for the round-2 minor findings from F165 onward Opus agents doing refutation, adjudication and judgement in one pass, four findings each. The minor findings of class *soft* were not judged and are reported here as wording suggestions (§3b). This synthesis is by Fable.

A pair of numbers counts as inconsistent only when it describes the same quantity under the same conditions (beam configuration, optics, isotope, programme year, luminosity share, estimator, truth vs reconstructed level); a figure quoted as history in an Appendix A revision row, or labelled superseded, is not a live claim. "Contradiction" below means two live statements of one quantity disagree, or a live statement disagrees with the current script output; "defect" means a single passage is wrong, unqualified, mis-attributed or mislabelled without a second number opposing it; "author decision" means the text cannot be fixed from the repository and a question is put to the authors.
